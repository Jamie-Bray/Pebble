# Launch Marketing Hooks: TikTok, Reels and Shorts

Last updated: 3 October 2026

For a solo founder with no budget. Every concept below can be filmed on one
phone in a normal home in under 15 minutes, using Pebble on screen.

## Who this is for, and how to talk to them

Videos of people taking 100 photos of their hair straighteners get
100k-200k likes because viewers recognise themselves. Some of them live with
OCD or checking anxiety, and for some of them it is hard. So:

- **Laugh with, never at.** The joke is "we all do this", never "look how
  weird this person is". Do not stitch or duet someone's video to make fun of
  them.
- **No medical claims, ever.** Do not say Pebble helps, treats, fixes or
  reduces OCD or anxiety. Do not say "break the loop", "stop checking",
  "never worry again", "peace of mind guaranteed", or "you're safe now". Do
  not use #OCD or #anxiety as hashtags to reach people. Pebble is a checklist
  that saves a photo and the time. That is the whole pitch.
- **Don't promise the photo settles it.** For some people, looking at a photo
  again becomes another check. Show Pebble as a record you can look back at,
  not a cure for the doubt.
- **If someone shares that they struggle** in the comments, reply kindly and
  briefly. For example: "That sounds really tiring. If it's taking over your
  day, a GP or doctor can help. Pebble is just a checklist, but I hope it's
  useful." Do not argue or pitch.
- **Film only your own home.** No house numbers, street names, car plates,
  keys up close, or other people's faces. Use a sample routine in the app.
- **Say "photo", not "evidence" or "proof you did it".** "Proof photos" is
  fine only as the feature name.

Posting plan: one video a day for the first two weeks, then three a week.
Reuse the best hook as a reply-to-comment video. Put the store link in your
bio once the app is live, and "Pebble Routines" in on-screen text so people
can search for it.

## 10 video concepts

### 1. "The 100-photo camera roll"
- **Hook (on screen, first 2 seconds):** "My camera roll is 40% hair straighteners"
- **Shots:** scroll a camera roll full of near-identical straightener photos
  (staged on your own phone). Cut to Pebble: run "Leaving the house", take one
  photo on the straighteners step, and the step shows the time.
- **Voiceover or text:** "Now it's one photo, saved with the time, in a list
  that tells me what I've already checked."
- **Caption:** "Anyone else? #hairstraighteners #didileaveiton #leavingthehouse"

### 2. "Turning back at the front door"
- **Hook:** "POV: you're at the bus stop and you can't remember locking the door"
- **Shots:** walking away, stopping, turning round. Freeze-frame. Rewind
  effect to 10 minutes earlier: tapping through "Front door locked" in
  Pebble. Then open History at the bus stop: "Front door locked, 08:02".
- **Caption:** "The 08:02 is the bit I needed. #didilockthedoor"

### 3. "Things I check before I leave, ranked"
- **Hook:** "Ranking the things I check before leaving the house"
- **Shots:** quick tier list with your hand pointing: straighteners, hob or
  stove, back door, windows, iron, heater, the cat. End on the routine built
  in Pebble from exactly that list.
- **Caption:** "What's on yours? I'll add the top answer to the starter list."
  (This invites comments, and you get material for the next video.)

### 4. "Reply to a comment: what's the app?"
- **Hook:** reply to the first comment that asks "what app is this?"
- **Shots:** 20-second screen recording: create a routine, add three steps,
  set "Take a photo" on one, run it.
- **Text:** "Free, no account needed, no ads."
- **Caption:** "Pebble Routines. It's just a checklist that saves the time."

### 5. "The straightener step, slow and satisfying"
- **Hook:** "The most satisfying 6 seconds of my morning"
- **Shots:** close-up, with a crisp sound: unplug, coil the cable, put the
  straighteners on the heat mat, phone photo, step goes green in Pebble. Use
  the app's completion sound.
- **Caption:** "Unplug. Photo. Done. #morningroutine #grwm"

### 6. "Builder's tour: solo founder"
- **Hook:** "I built an app because I kept photographing my straighteners"
- **Shots:** you at a desk or kitchen table, phone in hand. Honest 30-second
  story: why you made it, what it does, what it doesn't do ("it's not
  therapy, it's a checklist").
- **Caption:** "Solo dev, no ads, no data selling. Feedback welcome."
  (Founder stories work well with no budget. Be real, show your face if
  you're comfortable.)

### 7. "Holiday edition: the big trip shutdown"
- **Hook:** "The 5am taxi is outside and you're checking the oven for the third time"
- **Shots:** suitcase by the door, run Pebble's "Big Trip Home Shutdown"
  template: fridge, waste out, water, thermostat, plugs. Last step: front
  door.
- **Caption:** "Saving this one for every trip. #travelhack #leavingforholiday"
  (Post before half-term and summer holidays.)

### 8. "Hotel checkout sweep"
- **Hook:** "Every charger I've ever lost is in a hotel room"
- **Shots:** run the "Hotel Checkout Sweep" template: safe, outlets, under the
  bed, wardrobe, bedside drawer. Photo of the empty safe.
- **Caption:** "Check the safe. Every time. #travelhacks #hotel"
  (This broadens the audience beyond home checks and is lighter in tone.)

### 9. "Wait, did I...? (car edition)"
- **Hook:** "Walking back across the car park to check the car is locked"
- **Shots:** "Car Lock & Parking Check": windows up, no valuables showing,
  lock and listen for the clack, photo of the parking spot. Blur the plate.
- **Caption:** "Bonus: you'll know where you parked. #parkinghack"

### 10. "Bedtime house check, in the dark"
- **Hook:** "Lying in bed wondering if the back door's locked"
- **Shots:** dim lights, Pebble in a dark theme. "Bedtime House Check": back
  door, ground-floor windows, stove off, front door. Then into bed and check
  History once: everything listed with times.
- **Caption:** "Done before bed, so it's on the list when I wonder later.
  #nightroutine"

## Hooks to test as text-only posts

Use these over a single still of the app or a stock-style clip of a front
door. Keep the ones that get the most watch time and rewrite them for a full
video.

- "Did I lock the door? (asking for every day of my life)"
- "Straighteners: off. Photo: taken. Time: 08:02."
- "My leaving-the-house checklist, in the order I actually do it"
- "Nobody: ... Me at the front door: one more check"
- "Put the list on your phone, not in your head"

## Free channels besides short video

- **Reddit:** answer genuine "how do you remember if you locked the door"
  threads in r/LifeProTips or r/organization (helpful tips first, mention Pebble only if
  it fits, and follow each subreddit's self-promotion rules). Do not post in
  OCD support communities to promote the app.
- **Pinterest:** pin the "leaving the house checklist" as an image. Pins keep
  getting found for months.
- **Your own story posts:** short build-in-public updates (new template, a
  bug fixed because someone commented).
