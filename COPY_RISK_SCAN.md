# Pebble Copy-Risk Scan

Last scanned: May 4, 2026

The main copy risk is overpromising around safety, health, proof, backup, and
workplace compliance. Public store copy, onboarding, paywalls, screenshots, and
legal pages should describe Pebble as routine support and personal reassurance,
not treatment, diagnosis, emergency response, legal evidence, or guaranteed
storage.

## Phrases Softened In This Pass

- Replaced "photo evidence is stored safely" with short-term reassurance backup
  wording.
- Replaced "safely backed up and ready on any device" with supported backup and
  restore wording.
- Replaced "protected across devices" with "supported routine data" backup
  wording.
- Replaced "local data is still safe" with "local data remains on this device."
- Replaced "routine is now protected" with device reminder scheduling wording.
- Replaced "camera roll is never accessed" with "camera roll is not scanned,"
  because the app can let users choose a proof photo from their library.
- Replaced "available across your devices" with "backed up for supported
  restore."

## Phrases To Avoid In Store Listings And Screenshots

- "proof you are safe"
- "guaranteed backup"
- "never worry again"
- "break the OCD loop"
- "treat checking anxiety"
- "medical reassurance"
- "evidence vault"
- "workplace compliance proof"
- "manager safety system"
- "emergency alarm"

## Remaining Intentional Product Language

- "Proof photo" remains as a feature label because the app captures optional
  routine proof photos. Do not describe proof photos as legal evidence,
  workplace compliance evidence, or guaranteed safety evidence.
- "Cloud Vault" may remain only if nearby copy says it means short-term
  reassurance backup, not permanent archive storage or evidence storage.
- Template content may reference anxiety-adjacent routines because users may
  create those routines, but marketing should not claim diagnosis, treatment,
  prevention, monitoring, or clinical benefit.

## Next Store-Copy Review

- Review every Play Store listing paragraph, screenshot caption, paywall image,
  onboarding panel, website hero, and ad/social post against the avoid list
  above before publication.
