Account Deletion

How to delete your Pebble account.

This page explains how to delete your Pebble Routines account and what happens to your account, cloud backup data, local device data, and app-store subscription.

Contact: support@pebbleroutines.app
support@pebbleroutines.app

Delete your account in the app

If you can still access Pebble, the fastest method is to delete your account in the app:

  1. Open Your account in Pebble.
  2. Scroll to the account section.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion request.

Delete your account without the app

If you cannot access Pebble, you can request deletion with this form or by emailing support@pebbleroutines.app.

To help us process the request safely, send the request from the email address associated with your Pebble account if possible, and include:

  • the subject line Pebble account deletion request
  • the email address linked to the account
  • any details that help us identify the account

Email link: support@pebbleroutines.app

What we delete

When your Pebble account is deleted, we will delete or irreversibly de-identify the cloud account data and cloud backup data associated with that account, except where limited retention is required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, tax, accounting, dispute, or backup-retention reasons.

Pebble proof-photo backup is designed around a short rolling retention window. Premium cloud proof-photo copies are normally kept for up to 21 days unless you delete them earlier.

Active cloud account data is normally deleted within 30 days. Backup copies may remain for up to 90 days before automatic expiry, unless longer retention is required by law, security, fraud prevention, accounting, or dispute handling.

What may remain on your device

Pebble is local-first. Deleting your account does not necessarily remove routines, history, proof photos, guidance audio, drafts, settings, or other app data already saved locally on your device. You may need to delete that data manually, clear app data, or uninstall the app if you want local device data removed as well.

Pebble only deletes its own private app copies. It does not delete photos from your camera roll or photo library.

Subscription cancellation

Deleting your Pebble account, uninstalling the app, or signing out does not automatically cancel a subscription billed through an app store. To stop future renewals, you must cancel through the relevant app-store subscription settings.
