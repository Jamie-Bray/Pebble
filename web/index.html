What Pebble helps with

Everyday checks, easier to verify.

Leaving-home checks

Keep a simple sequence for the doors, keys, appliances, bags, or other checks you want to remember before you leave.

Optional records

Add notes, history, and optional photo confirmation when a visual record would help you check back later.

Reusable checklists

Create reusable routines with reminders and clear completion states, without turning your day into a dashboard.

How it works

Set it up once. Use it when the moment comes.

1

Privacy-first

Your routines are personal. Pebble keeps them that way.

Pebble is designed for local-first use, private-by-default settings, and clear choices before account or backup features are used. We do not sell personal data, use routine content for ads, or use your private routine content to train AI models.
