Privacy Policy

What Pebble collects, and why

Pebble Routines is a routine app that keeps your data on your phone by default. Most routine content stays there unless you choose to use an account or cloud backup.

Last updated: 3 October 2026
Contact: privacy@pebbleroutines.com

Short version Pebble does not sell your personal data, show ads, use your routines or proof photos for ads, or use your private routine content to train AI models. There is no ad SDK and no behavioural analytics SDK in the app.

Local-first use

You can use Pebble without an account. In local-only use, Pebble stores routine titles, steps, reminders, settings, history, optional proof photos, and guidance-audio recordings on your device.

Reminders are scheduled by your device's operating system. Pebble does not use a push-notification server. On Android, the home-screen widget shows routine details that Pebble saves on your device for the widget.

Free local history and Pebble-owned proof-photo copies are kept for a short recent-history window. Pebble only removes its own private app copies; it does not delete photos from your camera roll or photo library.

Local data may be lost if you delete the app, clear app data, lose your device, change devices before backup completes, or your device storage fails. Pebble is routine support, not permanent archive storage.

Accounts and backup

Signing in is optional. You can sign in with an email one-time code, Google, or Apple where available. If you sign in, Pebble processes your email address, account ID, sign-in provider, authentication session, and sign-in times.

Pebble asks Google and Apple for your email address only. Google may still include your name and profile picture link in the sign-in details it returns, and our sign-in provider may store them with your account record. Pebble does not use or show them. Apple may give us a private relay email address instead of your real one.

Like most online services, our sign-in and database provider records technical details such as IP address and device or browser information when your app connects, for security and abuse prevention.

Premium cloud backup requires an active paid subscription, sign-in, and your explicit choice to turn backup on. If enabled, Pebble may back up supported routine data, including routines, reminders, recent history, proof-photo files, and proof-photo records. Guidance audio files currently stay on your device, though routine backup may include related details such as filename, duration, MIME type, or byte size.

Before cloud backup uploads supported routine data, Pebble asks you to confirm that backup may include sensitive user-added content. We keep a record of that choice, including when you made it and the app, privacy, and terms versions shown at the time.

Subscriptions

Personal Premium is sold as a monthly or yearly subscription through Google Play or the App Store. The store handles payment. Pebble never receives your card or bank details.

We use RevenueCat to check subscription status and restore purchases. RevenueCat receives an app user ID (a random ID before you sign in, then your Pebble account ID), your purchase history for Pebble (product, purchase and renewal dates, status, and store transaction details), and basic device and app information such as platform, operating system version, and app version.

Pebble also keeps a subscription record with your account: the product, store, status, subscription period, and a one-way hash of the store purchase token. This is used to unlock Premium cloud features and to stop one purchase being claimed by several accounts.

Completion emails

Signed-in Premium users can choose one email contact for a routine. Pebble first emails that person an invitation. Pebble only sends completion emails after they accept, and every email includes links to stop the emails or block the sender.

The invitation and every completion email show the sender's account email address, so the person knows who they are from. A completion email can include the routine name, completion time, and step count. The sender can hide the routine name. Photos and checklist details are not included.

To do this, Pebble stores the contact's email address, invitation status, and email preferences, plus a log of emails sent (completion time, step counts, delivery status, and the routine name only if it was shown). Emails are delivered by Resend.

If a contact declines or stops the emails, the sender cannot invite them again for 30 days. If a contact blocks the sender, Pebble keeps their email address and a hashed copy linked to the sender's account so the sender cannot invite them again. A contact can also block invitations from everyone using Pebble; for this Pebble keeps only a hashed copy of their address, not linked to any account.

Only add someone who knows you and expects these emails. If you received a Pebble email you did not expect, you can block it from the email or contact privacy@pebbleroutines.com.

Crash reports

Release builds may use Sentry to receive crash reports, so we can find and fix problems. A crash report contains technical details: the error and stack trace, device model, operating system version, app version and build, and a short trail of recent technical app events. That trail can include internal technical identifiers.

Crash reporting is set up so it does not send your name, email address, routine content, photos, or audio. Pebble removes user details from app crash reports before they are sent, and does not use screenshots, session replay, or performance tracing.

Sensitive content you choose to add

Pebble does not ask for medical records or clinical labels. The routines, notes, reminders, photos, audio, and history you choose to create may still reveal private details about your home, family, workplace, habits, health, or personal circumstances.

Please avoid capturing other people, children, private documents, addresses, financial information, or sensitive third-party details in proof photos unless you have permission and a good reason.

Device permissions

  • Camera is used when you choose to capture a proof photo.
  • Photos or media library is used when you choose an existing proof photo, or save a copy to Photos. Pebble does not scan your library.
  • Microphone is used when you choose to record short guidance audio.
  • Notifications are used for routine reminders you set.

Pebble does not request location, contacts, SMS, or phone permissions.

Why we use data

Where UK or EU data protection law applies, we rely on these legal bases:

  • Contract: to provide sign-in, Premium, backup, restore, account deletion, and the features you ask for.
  • Consent: for cloud backup of content you add (which may include health or other sensitive details) and for completion emails. You can withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legitimate interests: to keep Pebble secure, fix crashes, prevent purchase misuse, and stop unwanted invitation emails.
  • Legal obligation: to keep records we must keep for tax, accounting, or legal reasons.

Service providers

We use providers only where needed to run Pebble. Supabase, RevenueCat, Sentry, and Resend process data on our behalf under their data processing terms. Google and Apple handle sign-in and payments under their own privacy policies.

  • Supabase for sign-in, database, private photo storage, server functions, and account deletion requests. Pebble's Supabase project is hosted in the USA.
  • RevenueCat (USA) for subscription status, purchase restore, and purchase history.
  • Google for Google sign-in and Google Play billing.
  • Apple for Sign in with Apple and App Store billing where available.
  • Sentry (USA) for crash reports, in builds where crash reporting is turned on.
  • Resend (USA) for delivering completion emails and their invitations.
  • Email and support tools when you contact us, and the email service that sends sign-in codes.

App stores and operating systems may also provide their own store analytics, payment records, or diagnostics under their own privacy policies.

International transfers

Pebble is run from the United Kingdom. If you sign in, back up, subscribe, send completion emails, or a crash report is sent, your data is processed in the USA by the providers listed above.

Where UK or EU law requires it, we rely on safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or Standard Contractual Clauses in our providers' data processing terms, or the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where a provider is certified. Contact us for more detail.

Children

Pebble is not designed for children and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly create accounts for children under 13. If you think a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive a copy of your personal data. You may also withdraw cloud-backup consent by pausing cloud backup in the app, deleting your account, or contacting us.

You can request account deletion through the account deletion page. The web form collects the email address you enter, your optional message, a hashed form of your IP address, and your browser's user agent, so we can find the account and prevent abuse.

If you are in the UK, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk. In the EU, you can contact your local data protection authority. We would like the chance to help first, so please contact us.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Pebble uses personal data, we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will also tell you in the app before it applies.
