Support

Help with Pebble Routines.

Questions about routines, reminders, optional photo confirmation, accounts, backup, or privacy requests can be sent to support.

Email: support@pebbleroutines.app

General support

For routine setup, reminders, app questions, or anything that is not working as expected.

Privacy requests

For access, correction, deletion, backup consent, or data questions.

Account deletion

Request deletion of cloud account data and cloud backup data.

What to include

  • The email address linked to your Pebble account, if you use one.
  • Your device type and operating system version, if relevant.
  • A short description of what happened and what you expected.
  • Whether the issue involves local-only use, account sign-in, or cloud backup.

Important note

Pebble is for routine checks and optional records. It is not an emergency, medical, clinical, workplace safety, or legal evidence service. If something is urgent or safety-critical, use the appropriate local service or professional support.
